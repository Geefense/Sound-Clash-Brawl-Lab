The 68 px is a diagnostic, not a slide measurement. It is the difference between how far the planted foot travels through the frame in each half of the cycle at one uniform scale. How much a foot actually appears to skate depends on the cadence and on the forward speed the game drives the character at — a slow cycle over a fast world speed skates more, whatever these numbers are. Read it as "the two halves disagree by about this much", not "the foot slides 68 px". What this comparison does not fix: the corrected key's trousers are PASS A's, so the near leg is still PASS A's; swapping which leg is near needs a redraw, as does the free arm, which leads in all four keys here — its swing is deliberately left as drawn so the comparison isolates the pass phase. The microphone is in the right hand in every key, corrected one included. Nothing here is wired into gameplay.
